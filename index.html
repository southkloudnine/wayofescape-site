Way of Escape

Break a habit with daily prayer and methods grounded in research. One habit at a time, one day at a time.

  • Start and end each day in prayer

    A prayer at each end of your day, and clear steps in between.

  • Use methods backed by research

    If–then plans, trigger removal, urge surfing and more.

  • Find grace when you slip

    A slip is not the end. Get up and keep going. Slips never erase your progress.

Private by design

Everything you write and track stays on your phone. There's no account to make, nothing is sent to us, and there are no ads or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
