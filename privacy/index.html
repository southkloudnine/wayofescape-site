Privacy policy

Your journey stays on your phone

Last updated November 23, 2026

Way of Escape doesn't collect, store or share any of your information. We don't have servers that hold your data, and the app doesn't connect to the internet.

What the app keeps, and where

To help you, the app saves what you put into it: the habit you're breaking, your plan, your days, prayers and steps, urges and slips, ratings, notes and journal entries, and your accountability partner's name and phone number if you add one. All of it is stored only on your device, inside the app.

We, the makers of Way of Escape, never receive any of it. We can't see it, and we couldn't recover it for you if it were lost.

Like other apps, this information is included in the backups your device makes (to iCloud or a computer), if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It doesn't connect to the internet. The Bible verses ship inside the app.
  • It has no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and no third-party code that does.
  • It doesn't sell, rent or share information, because it never has any to share.

Permissions the app may ask for

Each of these is optional, asked only when you use the feature, and can be changed at any time in your device's Settings.

Notifications

Reminders for your morning and evening prayers and check-ins. They're scheduled on your device. Reminder text never names your habit, so it stays private on your lock screen.

Face ID or passcode

If you turn on the lock, the app uses Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to keep your notes and progress private. Your face or fingerprint data is handled by your device and is never available to the app or to us.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you tap the microphone in a writing field, the app listens only until you tap stop, and turns what you say into text on your device. Speech recognition is required to run on the device itself, so nothing you say is sent anywhere, and no recording is kept.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it. For example:

  • Calling or texting your partner opens your Phone or Messages app with a message ready. You decide whether to send it.
  • Sharing notes, or exporting My journey as a PDF, opens the share sheet. The file goes only where you send it.

Once you send something, it's handled by the app or person you sent it to.

Deleting your information

In the app, go to Me › Erase everything and start over. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

Way of Escape isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever begins to collect information, the policy will say so clearly before that happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@wayofescape.app.

Way of Escape is free, with no ads, accounts or tracking.

Way of Escape supports your effort. It does not replace medical or professional care.
