About the creator

I'm just a guy who is in absolute love with Jesus.

I wasn't always. Through my youth I was addicted to many things: marijuana, tobacco, alcohol and other drugs. I was in and out of jail. Even then, God was leading me, protecting me and keeping me through more situations than I can count.

In the summer of 2007 I quit weed cold turkey, and I haven't smoked a joint since. In March 2008 I was saved and baptized. The road has been bumpy since, but my life has never been the same.

In 2016 I threw half a pack of cigarettes into a local bar's garbage can, and I haven't smoked a cigarette or a cigar since. Alcohol has been harder. I've quit for more than a year on two separate occasions, and I'm still walking that road.

So I didn't make this app from a finish line. I use these same methods myself, and I'm using Way of Escape for my own struggles right now. I made it for me and for people like me, who struggle with sin.

God has given me a beautiful wife and five amazing children. We all love the Lord, and we're members of a local church.

The app's name comes from a promise:

“No temptation has overtaken you that is not common to man. God is faithful, and he will not let you be tempted beyond your ability, but with the temptation he will also provide the way of escape, that you may be able to endure it.”

1 Corinthians 10:13 ESV

It's my passion to help show people that way of escape. There is only one, and His name is Jesus, who breaks every chain. This app can't do that. It's a tool to help you pedal while He leads. Amen.

What worked for me isn't medical advice. Stopping alcohol and some drugs suddenly can be dangerous, so please talk with a doctor before you do.

Way of Escape is free, with no ads, accounts or tracking.

Way of Escape supports your effort. It does not replace medical or professional care.
