Following Jesus

Curious about following Jesus?

This app is built on the good news about Jesus. If you've wondered what that is, here it is in a few verses. No pressure, and nothing to sign up for.

We all fall short

Every one of us has turned away from God, in ways big and small. The habit you're fighting is one part of a bigger story.

“for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,”

Romans 3:23 ESV

God loves us anyway

God didn't wait for us to clean ourselves up. He sent His Son for us while we were still far from Him.

“but God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.”

Romans 5:8 ESV

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life.”

John 3:16 ESV

A free gift

Forgiveness and new life can't be earned. They're a gift, received by trusting Jesus.

“For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.”

Romans 6:23 ESV

“For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast.”

Ephesians 2:8-9 ESV

Turn and trust

To repent is to turn around: away from sin, and toward God. To believe is to put your trust in Jesus, who died for you and rose again.

“and saying, “The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand; repent and believe in the gospel.”

Mark 1:15 ESV

“because, if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.”

Romans 10:9 ESV

A new start

Anyone who is in Christ is made new. When you fall, you don't start over alone. Confess it, and come back to Him.

“Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come.”

2 Corinthians 5:17 ESV

“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.”

1 John 1:9 ESV

A prayer, if you're ready

Lord Jesus, I know I have sinned and fallen short of Your glory. I believe You died for my sins and rose again. I turn from my sin, and I put my trust in You. Forgive me, make me new, and lead my life from today. Thank You for Your grace. Amen.

These words don't save you. Jesus does. If they say what's in your heart, pray them to Him in your own way.

Where to go from here

  • Read the Gospel of John, a chapter a day.
  • Find a church near you that teaches the Bible, and go this Sunday.
  • Tell a Christian friend, or your partner, what you've decided.
  • Keep praying each morning and evening, in Way of Escape or wherever you are. He hears you.
