If you need help right now

If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US), any time. In an emergency, call 911.

For substance use, the SAMHSA National Helpline is free, confidential and open all day, every day: 1-800-662-4357.

Stopping some substances suddenly, including alcohol, can be dangerous. Talk with a doctor before you change how much you use.

Common questions

Is Way of Escape really free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads, subscriptions, sponsorships or in-app purchases.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your device. There's no account, and nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Your progress is part of your device backup. If you restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, your journey comes with it. The app doesn't sync between devices on its own.

What happens if I slip?

Slips never reset your progress. The day is marked "restored," the app helps you see what led to it, and you keep going. You can also adjust your plan right there.

Can I work on more than one habit?

Yes, up to three at a time. Go to Me › Add another habit.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Change your prayer times in Me. To turn notifications off or back on, go to your device's Settings › Notifications › Way of Escape.

The microphone button doesn't appear.

Speaking instead of typing works only where your device can turn speech into text without a connection, in your language. If your device can't, the button is hidden. If you said no to microphone or speech recognition access, you can turn it back on in Settings › Way of Escape.

How do I share my progress with a counselor or pastor?

Go to Me › View my journey, then Export as PDF, and send it however you like.

How do I erase everything?

Go to Me › Erase everything and start over. This erases your commitments, plan and whole journey on this device and can't be undone.

Is this a treatment for addiction?

No. Way of Escape supports your effort with prayer and practical methods. It doesn't replace medical or professional care. If you're dealing with a dependency, please talk with a doctor or counselor.
